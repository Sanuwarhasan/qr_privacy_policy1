Privacy Policy — QR Studio

App ID: com.ssragency.qrstudio.vxkmpr • Effective Date: November 24, 2026

In short: QR Studio scans and generates QR codes and barcodes entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information, camera feed, photos, or scan history.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

QR Studio is designed to work completely offline without user accounts or cloud servers. We do not collect or store:

  • Camera video frames, images, or biometric data
  • Scanned or generated QR code contents (URLs, Wi-Fi passwords, contacts, or text)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, precise location, or usage analytics

2. Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly to detect QR codes and barcodes in real time via on-device CameraX and Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning. Camera frames are processed transiently in memory and are never recorded or uploaded.
  • Photo Selection (Android Photo Picker): When you choose to scan a QR code from an image, QR Studio uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker so the app only reads the single image you explicitly select. QR Studio does not request broad storage or media library permissions.

3. Local Data Storage

Your scanned and created QR code history (along with starred items) is stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database (Room).

  • You can delete any individual entry or clear unstarred history directly inside the History tab.
  • Uninstalling QR Studio permanently deletes all locally stored history from your device.

4. Third-Party Links & Actions

When you choose to open a scanned website, connect to Wi-Fi settings, dial a phone number, send an email or SMS, add a contact, or share a generated QR PNG image, Android hands that action off to the app or system panel you select. Those external apps and websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

QR Studio does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of QR Studio, the updated version will be included directly inside the app and posted with a revised effective date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at support@qrstudio.app.
